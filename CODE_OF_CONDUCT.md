Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct (v2.1)

Our project follows the Contributor Covenant v2.1 to foster an open and welcoming community.

Summary
- Be kind, respectful, and patient.
- Assume good faith and be welcoming to newcomers.
- Do not harass, discriminate against, or abuse other contributors.

Reporting
If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, please contact the maintainers so we can address it.

For the full text and details, see: https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/
